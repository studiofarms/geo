GOCOACH — EXECUTIVE GROUP COACHING
Carol Anthony, M.S., PCC

A practice for forward motion.

THE COHORT
6–7 mid-level managers · 6 sessions of 90 minutes · 12 weeks
Investment: $1,200 per seat for the program.

WEEK 01 / SESSION 01 — BASELINE
The map before the work. Each participant arrives with a 360 read and a
presenting problem. Establish the starting point and outcomes to work toward.

WEEK 03 / SESSION 02 — THE INTERIOR WORK
Surface the patterns a manager cannot see in themselves. Find language for
the meeting, conversation, or challenge that keeps recurring.

WEEK 05 / SESSION 03 — UNDER PRESSURE
Working through change. Live cases brought by participants and worked in
real time, with attention to leading people through uncertainty.

WEEK 07 / SESSION 04 — ACROSS
Collaboration without authority. Cross-team rehearsals, practiced in pairs.

WEEK 09 / SESSION 05 — WHO STAYS
Retention as a practice. Work through the conversations managers should be
having with their direct reports, using real situations in private.

WEEK 12 / SESSION 06 — INTEGRATION
What changed and what comes next. Revisit the baseline and complete a written
practice plan to share with the participant's sponsor.

CONFIDENTIALITY
Sponsors see outcomes and practice plans, not transcripts of conversations.
Exact dates and engagement details are agreed in the initial conversation.
