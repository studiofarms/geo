The program · A working syllabus

Six sessions.
A practice you keep.

Twelve weeks of real cases, peer reflection, and deliberate practice. Led by Carol Anthony, M.S., PCC.

Inquire about a cohort ↗Download syllabus ↓
Cohort
6–7 managers
Sessions
6 × 90 minutes
Duration
12 weeks
Investment
$1,200 per seat
  1. Week 01
    Session 01

    Baseline

    The map before the work. Each participant arrives with a 360 read and a presenting problem of their own. Establish a starting point and the outcomes to work toward.

  2. Week 03
    Session 02

    The interior work

    Surface the pattern a manager cannot see in themselves: the meeting they always lose or the conversation they keep avoiding. Find language for it and a different way to respond.

  3. Week 05
    Session 03

    Under pressure

    Working through change. Live cases brought by participants, worked in real time. Practice leading people through a reorganization, acquisition, or system migration.

  4. Week 07
    Session 04

    Across

    Collaboration without authority. Cross-team rehearsals, practiced in pairs. Work on outcomes that depend on people and teams you do not directly manage.

  5. Week 09
    Session 05

    Who stays

    Retention as a practice. Work through the conversations a manager should be having with each direct report, using real situations in the privacy of the cohort.

  6. Week 12
    Session 06

    Integration

    What changed, and what is next. Return to the baseline and complete a written practice plan, shared with each participant’s sponsor.

The work stays with the group. Sponsors see outcomes and practice plans, rather than transcripts of confidential conversations. The exact calendar and engagement details are agreed during the initial conversation.
