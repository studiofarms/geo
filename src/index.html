§ 01 · A note on the form
Deliberate.

Group coaching is not the discount form of executive coaching. It is the form managers actually need: five or six other people in the same chair, doing the same work, on the same arc. Confidence is built in company. Patterns are seen by other people first. And the practice — when it lands — keeps moving long after the twelve weeks are over.

— The practice, in one paragraph.

§ 02 · The four studies

Four practices,
in motion together.

Every cohort moves through four working areas. They aren't modules. They're directions of attention — recurring across the six sessions, surfacing where the cohort needs them.

No. 01 · Coaching

The interior work.

We surface the pattern a manager can't see in themselves — the meeting they always lose, the conversation they keep avoiding — and give them language for it.

Recurs in all six sessions
No. 02 · Change Management

Movement under pressure.

How to lead people through a reorganization, an acquisition, a system migration — without losing the people who matter most on the way through.

Live cases · session 03
No. 03 · Collaboration

The cross-functional middle.

Where most managers' work actually happens — across teams they don't own, with stakeholders who don't report to them, on outcomes that require both.

Practiced in pairs · Session 04
No. 04 · Talent Retention

Why people stay.

The seven conversations a manager should be having with each direct report — and the one most are missing entirely. Worked through with real names, in private.

Worked privately · session 05
§ 03 · The method

Six sessions.
Twelve weeks.
Visible movement.

Each cohort follows the same arc. We open with a baseline measurement, run four working sessions on the four practices, and close with an integration session and a written practice plan that the participant shares with their sponsor.

  1. Wk 01 · Session 01

    Baseline

    The map before the work. Each participant arrives with a 360 read and a presenting problem of their own.

  2. Wk 03 · Session 02

    The interior

    The pattern under the pattern. Coaching practice in front of, and from, the cohort.

  3. Wk 05 · Session 03

    Under pressure

    Working through change. Live cases brought in by participants, worked in real time.

  4. Wk 07 · Session 04

    Across

    Collaboration without authority. Cross-team rehearsals, practiced in pairs.

  5. Wk 09 · Session 05

    Who stays

    Retention as a practice, not a panic. The seven conversations, walked through.

  6. Wk 12 · Session 06

    Integration

    What changed. What's next. The written practice plan, shared with each participant's sponsor.

§ 05 · Who it's for

For the people paying.

L&D & HR sponsors

Coaching that scales without thinning.

  • i.An ICF-credentialed coach, not a facilitator running someone else's curriculum. Carol holds PCC — the second-highest tier in the field.
  • ii.Six to seven seats, deep engagement at $1,200 per participant for the full twelve-week engagement.
  • iii.Manager-level reporting. Sponsor briefings at week 01 and week 12. Outcome deltas in writing, by participant, against the baseline 360.
  • iv.Stable scheduling. Six 90-minute sessions on a fixed cadence. No rescheduling chaos. Calendars locked at kickoff.
  • v.Designed for mid-level managers — the layer most coaching budgets miss, where the leverage on retention and productivity is highest.

For the people participating.

Enrolled participants

An hour and a half that you'll keep.

  • i.Five or six other people in the same seat. Not a generic leadership class. Not strangers role-playing scenarios.
  • ii.Real cases, not case studies. What you bring is what we work on — your meeting, your direct report, your stuck conversation.
  • iii.Confidential to the cohort. What's said in the room doesn't go to your sponsor. The sponsor sees outcomes, not transcripts.
  • iv.A written practice plan. You leave with something specific you'll actually keep — and someone in the cohort to compare notes with at week 26.
  • v.Six sessions of ninety minutes. Not nine months of weekly check-ins. Not a homework PDF. Time you can defend on your calendar.
